\section{Performance Requirements}

Unless stated otherwise, the timing requirements in this section are measured
on a mid-range Android phone (one released within the last four years) with a
typical mobile data or Wi-Fi connection.

\subsection{Speed and Latency Requirements}

\begin{description}
  \item[PR-SL1: App launch]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The app shall show the home screen within 3 seconds of
    being opened from a closed state.
    \item[Rationale:] Users will mostly open the app for short check-ins, such
    as after a walk or workout. A slow start makes those check-ins less likely.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] Across 20 cold starts on the reference phone, the
    home screen appears in 3 seconds or less in at least 19 of them.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SL2: Response to input]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The app shall respond to a tap or navigation action
    within 1 second.
    \item[Rationale:] Supports the goal of a simple and delightful user
    experience. Delays longer than about a second make an app feel broken.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] At least 95\% of measured screen transitions and
    button responses complete within 1 second.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SL3: Trades]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] After a user confirms a buy or sell order, the updated
    holdings and currency balance shall be shown within 2 seconds.
    \item[Rationale:] Users need to see the result of a trade straight away to
    connect the action with its effect on their portfolio.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] At least 95\% of test trades show the updated
    portfolio within 2 seconds of confirmation.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SL4: Activity sync]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] When the app is opened, activity recorded since the
    last sync shall be read and turned into currency within 10 seconds.
    \item[Rationale:] The reward has to feel tied to the activity that earned
    it. The whole point of the product is to make a distant payoff feel
    immediate.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] In testing, the currency balance reflects all
    activity available in Health Connect within 10 seconds of opening the app.
  \end{description}
\end{description}

\subsection{Safety-Critical Requirements}

\progname{} does not control physical equipment, so none of its failures can
directly injure someone. The Hazard Analysis does identify ways the product
could encourage harm, and the requirements below address them. They will be
traced to the safety requirements in the Hazard Analysis once those are
finalized.

\begin{description}
  \item[PR-SC1: Daily earning cap]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The amount of currency a user can earn from activity
    in one day shall be capped.
    \item[Rationale:] Without a cap, the product would reward users for
    exercising past safe limits to earn more.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] No amount of activity recorded in a single day earns
    more than the daily cap.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SC2: No penalties for missed days]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The product shall not take away currency or holdings
    when a user misses a goal or a day of activity.
    \item[Rationale:] The Hazard Analysis lists shame over missed goals as a
    psychological hazard. Losing earned progress would make that worse, and
    could push users to exercise while injured or unwell.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] No feature of the product reduces a user's balance
    or holdings because of inactivity.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SC3: Simulated market is labelled]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] Every screen that shows prices, holdings, or returns
    shall state that the market is simulated and that results do not predict
    real returns.
    \item[Rationale:] The Hazard Analysis lists financial loss from users
    applying strategies from the app to real markets.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] A review of every market and portfolio screen finds
    the notice visible on each one.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SC4: Not financial advice]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The product shall state that its content is for
    education only and is not financial advice. The statement shall be shown
    during sign-up, where the user must acknowledge it before continuing, and
    shall stay available from the settings screen. Lessons, tips, and any other
    content that discusses investing shall not recommend specific real-world
    investments to a user.
    \item[Rationale:] The Hazard Analysis lists financial loss from users
    acting on what they learn in the app. \progname{} is not a licensed
    financial advisor, so users must understand that nothing in the app is
    personal advice about their real money.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] A new account cannot be created without
    acknowledging the statement, the statement can be reached from settings,
    and a review of all educational content finds no recommendations to buy
    or sell specific real-world investments.
  \end{description}

  \item[PR-SC5: Exercise injury warning]\leavevmode
  \begin{description}
    \item[Description:] The product shall warn users that physical activity
    carries a risk of injury, that they should exercise within their own
    limits, and that they should consult a doctor before starting a new
    exercise routine if they have health concerns. The product's terms of use
    shall state that users exercise at their own risk and that the
    \progname{} team is not responsible for any injury that happens while
    using the app. The user must acknowledge both during sign-up before
    continuing.
    \item[Rationale:] The product rewards physical activity, so some users
    will exercise more because of it. The Hazard Analysis assumes users only
    attempt exercises they are able to do safely, and places injuries from
    exercise outside the product's control. Users need to be told this
    directly. The responsibility statement is a legal matter as well as a
    safety one, so it should also be listed under the Legal Requirements in
    Compliance Requirements, and its exact wording should be checked against
    the course's guidance before release.
    \item[Fit Criterion:] A new account cannot be created without
    acknowledging the injury warning and the terms of use, and both can be
    reached from the settings screen at any time.
  \end{description}
\end{description}
