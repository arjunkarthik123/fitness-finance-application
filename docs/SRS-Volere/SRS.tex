\section{Tasks}

This section outlines the tasks that will be undertaken during the development of the solution. Each task is associated with a specific duration, start and finish date, and any dependencies on other tasks. The tasks are designed to ensure that the project progresses in a structured manner, with clear milestones and deliverables. This section also includes the phase-in plan identifying when requirements will be phased into the development process.

\subsection{Project Planning}

The team will follow an agile development process throughout the development of this solution, with a focus on iterative development and continuous feedback. The project will be divided into several sprints, each lasting two weeks. At the end of each sprint, the team will conduct a review to assess progress and make necessary adjustments. Each sprint will consist of planning, developing, testing, and deploying. This will allow the team to make iterative progress while still ensuring that previous iterations are effective and meet the requirements of the stakeholders.
The detailed breakdown of weekly tasks and deliverables can be found in \href{pdfs/DevelopmentPlan/DevelopmentPlan.pdf}{section 9 of the development plan} and is the basis for the following project task breakdown:
