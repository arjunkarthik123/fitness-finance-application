DOC-01 &
Financial Glossary and Tooltips &
\textbf{Description:} Flexin shall provide contextual tooltips and a glossary defining financial concepts, including compound interest, inflation, ETFs, bond yields, and portfolio diversification.

\textbf{Rationale:} Contextual explanations help novice users learn financial terminology while using the application.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} All financial instruments and portfolio metrics shall link to plain-language definitions. At least 90\% of novice test participants shall locate and understand an ETF or compound return definition within 15 seconds. \\
\hline

DOC-02 &
Fitness Integration Guide &
\textbf{Description:} Flexin shall provide an illustrated setup guide explaining how to authorize and configure supported fitness integrations and troubleshoot synchronization issues.

\textbf{Rationale:} Clear instructions help users connect their fitness data sources successfully.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} Screenshot-assisted instructions shall cover all supported providers. At least 95\% of test participants shall complete authorization using the guide alone. \\
\hline

DOC-03 &
Searchable Help Center &
\textbf{Description:} Flexin shall provide an in-app searchable FAQ covering activity rewards, simulated market mechanics, virtual currency disclaimers, and health-data privacy.

\textbf{Rationale:} Accessible documentation improves transparency, reduces confusion, and builds user trust.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} The help center shall operate without an external browser, support real-time keyword filtering, and return search results in under 200 milliseconds. \\
\hline

DOC-04 &
Digital Manual and Release Notes &
\textbf{Description:} The team shall maintain a digital user manual and release notes in the project GitHub documentation repository.

\textbf{Rationale:} Documentation enables supervisors, reviewers, and evaluators to verify functionality and assess project deliverables.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} A self-contained PDF and Markdown manual shall document architecture, navigation, data flows, and troubleshooting, with release notes for major project milestones. \\
