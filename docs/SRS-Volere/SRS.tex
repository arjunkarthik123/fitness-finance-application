LF-APP-01 &
Visual Hierarchy &
\textbf{Description:} The dashboard shall present fitness metrics and financial metrics in a balanced, uncluttered layout using unified summary cards.

\textbf{Rationale:} Equal emphasis reinforces the relationship between physical activity and simulated investment growth.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} All primary screens shall follow the Figma design library and its 4-point/8-point spacing grid. \\
\hline

LF-APP-02 &
Compounding Chart &
\textbf{Description:} The portfolio chart shall distinguish cumulative workout earnings from simulated investment gains and dividends.

\textbf{Rationale:} Users should understand the difference between contributions and compounded returns.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} Two visually distinct chart traces shall allow users to distinguish these values within 5 seconds. \\
\hline

LF-APP-03 &
Responsive Layout &
\textbf{Description:} The interface shall adapt to Android screen sizes, aspect ratios, pixel densities, and system font scaling.

\textbf{Rationale:} Flexin must remain usable across supported Android devices.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} On Android 10+ devices with 5.5--6.8-inch screens, no text shall clip or touch targets overlap, including at 130\% font scaling. \\
\hline

LF-APP-04 &
Theme and Contrast &
\textbf{Description:} The interface shall support Light and Dark modes with distinguishable fitness and financial status indicators.

\textbf{Rationale:} Consistent contrast improves readability and accessibility in different environments.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} The interface shall provide sufficient colour contrast for readability, meeting WCAG 2.1 AA minimum ratios of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and essential graphical elements. \\
\hline

LF-APP-05 &
Navigation &
\textbf{Description:} A persistent bottom navigation bar shall link to Dashboard, Activity, Market/Portfolio, Learn, and Community.

\textbf{Rationale:} Simple navigation reduces user effort.

\textbf{Fit Criterion:} Primary operations shall be reachable within three taps from the home dashboard. \\
