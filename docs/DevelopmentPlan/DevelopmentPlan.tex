\section{Team Meeting Plan}

When a lecture is held (where the professor is delivering material - primarily 
until October 1st, and a few times throughout the winter 2027 semester), the team 
will spend 5-10 minutes, post-lecture, informally meeting to touch base on the
progress made, and any roadblocks that may need to be addressed. These informal 
meetings will act as 'stand-up' style meetings where the team can hold each other
accountable for the continuous work they have completed and identify any roadblocks
in the development early so that they can be addressed with the help of other team 
members ahead of project deadlines. On days where there is no lecture, due to 
this time already being reserved in our calendars, the team will hold more formal  
meetings during these time slots to discuss in greater depth progress made, resolving 
any road blocks, and action items for each team member ahead of next meeting. These 
meetings will be held in-person on campus or virtually over Microsoft Teams. The 
exact location will be discussed and determined by the team on the Sunday at the 
start of each week, in order to best accommodate the schedules of all team members 
and to account for necessary accomodations for commuters. The scheduled lecture 
time used for meetings will occur every week on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays from 
5:30-6:20 PM.

During scheduled tutorial time (weekly on Wednesdays from 2:30-4:20 PM in the fall semester, 
and Tuesdays from 2:30-4:20 PM in the winter semester), the team will meet in person. 
The first 5-10 minutes will be spent preparing and reviewing the agenda for our 
discussion with the TA, 20 minutes will be dedicated to the discussion with the 
TA to receive feedback and ask any pending questions (note: our TA is also our 
supervisor, thus this time will also be served to address any requirements based 
questions), and then any remaining time will be dedicated to development time 
where the team will work on dicussing and/or implementing critical logic requires 
extra attention and alignment on.

In addition, the team will hold weekly meetings outside of class time (lectures and 
tutorials) every Tuesday from 10:00-11:00 AM. These meetings will be held virtually 
over Microsoft Teams. The puropse of these meetings will be to discuss upcoming 
deliverables and planning for the week ahead (i.e. this may include assigning action 
items for a project artifact).

All meetings are subject to rescheduling and/or cancellation based on discussion 
where all team members are in agreement, and will be communicated to all team 
members at least 24 hours in advance. Meeting rescheduling and/or cancellation 
may be due to holidays, McMaster University shutdown days, or group consensus to 
postpone a meeting (\textbf{excpetion}: tutorial meetings \underline{cannot} be rescheduled 
and/or cancelled due to group consenus as meeting w/ the TA is a mandatory and 
beneficial part of the development feedback process).

For each of the meetings mentioned above, an agenda will be determined at the 
end of the previous meeting. Each meeting will closely follow this agenda, leaving 
time at the end for any questions to be addressed. The roles for each meeting 
will remain consistent, as to maintain efficiency in the meetings. Each meeting 
Noah will serve as the meeting chair, responsible for leading the meeting, Daniel F. 
will serve as the notetaker, responsible for capturing talking points and recording 
action items, and [TBD] will be responsible for capturing meeting attendance as 
issues on GitHub. The meeting template (\href{pdfs/MeetingMinutesTemplate.pdf}{MeetingMinutesTemplate.pdf}) will be used to capture meeting minutes, 
agendas, action items, and other details about each meeting. These meeting records 
will be stored on the shared Microsoft Teams channel for the team for later reference.
