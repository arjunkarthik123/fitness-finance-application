\section{Introduction}

This document aims to illustrate the hazards that may occur during the development and deployment of Flexin'.
It defines hazards for this project, the purpose of analyzing them, and how the team will mitigate them to ensure safe use of Flexin'.

\subsection{Flexin' Problem Statement}

Personal finance and fitness follow the same pattern: the payoff for effort put
in today shows up years later. Someone who invests \$100 a month from age 25,
assuming a 7\% annual return, contributes \$48,000 over 40 years and ends up with
roughly \$262,000. Waiting until 35 to start the same plan leaves them with about
\$122,000. The same logic applies to exercise. A single walk or workout changes
very little, but the habit, kept up for years, is what separates a healthy
40-year-old from an unhealthy one. In both cases the early actions matter most,
and in both cases they are the easiest to skip, because the reward is too far
away to feel real.

The concepts behind this are not widely understood, and young people understand
them the least. A 2012 survey of 6,911 Canadians asked three basic questions
covering compound interest, inflation, and risk diversification~\cite{boisclair2017}.
Only 42\% of respondents answered all three correctly.
Among respondents under 35, that number fell to about 30\%, the lowest of any
age group, and 44\% of them answered ``don't know'' to at least one question.
Only about half of respondents under 35 knew that buying a single company's
stock is usually riskier than buying a stock mutual fund. The same study found
that respondents who answered all three questions correctly were 10 percentage
points more likely to have retirement savings, which suggests that this gap in
knowledge carries through to real financial outcomes.

Tools for practising each skill already exist, but separately. Stock market
simulators let people trade with fake money, and fitness apps reward steps with
points and badges. A market simulator only helps someone who is already
motivated to learn about investing, which excludes most of the young adults the
survey shows are furthest behind. Fitness rewards, meanwhile, rarely lead to
anything beyond the app itself. No product currently connects the two so that a
habit a user is already building (or wants to build) becomes their way into
learning how money grows.

\subsection{Hazard Analysis Introduction}

A hazard as it pertains to Flexin' is a condition in the software that can create an unwanted effect of event on Flexin's users, or the general environment.
In software, these hazards stem from poor design decisions or requirements.
These hazards must be addressed prior to and during development in order to limit any unwanted impact on users.

As described above, Flexin' focuses on developing healthy habits for investing and fitness and teach users the effect of compounding.
However, to accomplish this goal there are many hazards that may hurt users physically, psychologically, or financially.
These hazards may come from poor software implementation, incompatible data, or faulty integration with outside sources.
The hazard analysis document below aims to identify all hazards and how the team plans to address them.


\section{Scope and Purpose of Hazard Analysis}

The hazard analysis document outlines the hazards that may occur while using Flexin'.
The high level hazards in scope of Flexin' include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Physical/Psychological Harm}: Includes injuries that may occur during workouts that users intend to track.
    Also includes any impacts on mental health caused by the platform, such as addiction (similar to Instagram or TikTok) or feelings of embarrassment/shame for missing goals.
    \item \textbf{Financial Loss}: It is imperative to note that any interactions with financial markets on Flexin' are not indicative of real market results.
    Users may take strategies learned on the platform and incur serious financial loss.
    \item \textbf{Data Mismanagement}: Poor management of user data from database or cybersecurity perspectives may result in data leaks or loss of data.
    In addition, users improperly providing data can cause over/under rewarding of virtual currency, and can therefore lead to poor learning.
\end{itemize}

The purpose of the hazard analysis is to identify and mitigate risks that could affect the reliability, correctness and safety of the Flexin' app.
Conduction of this analysis will allow the team to anticipate failures early, and design around the hazards in a way that prioritizes the safety of the users.
The findings from this process will inform safety and security requirements, as well as having an impact on compliance and maintainability requirements.
In addition, this work will contribute to the creation of a fun, safe, and intuitive platform for users to learn about compounding and the importance of fitness and financial literacy.

\section{System Boundaries and Components}

Flexin' will consist of many key components, all of which will come together to create the product.
These components may not be separate entities, but they are distinct enough to present their own hazards.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 1: Account Creation \& Authentication}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Manages account creation and authentication for users
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Allow users to create accounts for Flexin'
            \item Populate the database with the user account to store the created account
            \item Store authentication key to ensure that only the account owner can log into their Flexin' account
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 2: User Information}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Manages user profile data such as name, height, weight, goals, workout data, etc.
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Read and write to the database with user information
            \item Provide profile data for users
            \item Allow users to set preferences within Flexin'
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 3: Fitness Device Integrations}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Allow Flexin' to connect with users' fitness devices
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Connect with users' devices
            \item Take data from said devices when relevant
            \item Ensure that connections with the devices are secure and reliable
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 4: Exercise Data}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Track and analyze users' exercise data
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Interact with the data collected from the fitness devices
            \item Analyze exercise data against user goals
            \item Read and write to the database with results and analytics
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 5: Currency Attribution}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Turn exercise results into virtual currency
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Use the analytics from exercise data
            \item Compute the correct amount of currency to reward users
            \item Read and write to the database with users' balances
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 6: Market Simulation}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} The underlying behaviour of the market in Flexin'
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Teach users about the effects of compounding
            \item Mimic proper stock market behaviour
            \item Incorporate randomness through seeding
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 7: Investment Platform}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Allow users to invest in the simulated market with their currency
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Provide users with securities to invest in
            \item Allow for buying and selling of said securities
            \item Read and write to the database with investment information
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 8: Social Management}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Create a space for users to compete with their friends on Flexin'
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Manage group creation, validation and invites
            \item Use the simulated markets randomness to create fair competition
            \item Read and write to the database with group information
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 9: Database}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} The single source of truth for all aspects of Flexin'
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item ACID database that will ensure all data is correct
            \item Accept reads and writes from components
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Component 10: User Interface \& Experience}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Purpose:} Provide users a place to interact with all backend components of Flexin'
        \item \textbf{Key Functions:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Manage all user facing screens, navigation, and input
            \item Ensure that components connect well to create a simple experience
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\section{Critical Assumptions}

The below assumptions define the scope of the hazard analysis by stating conditions that are expected to hold.
These conditions will allow the FMEA to go into depth about failure modes that Flexin' has control over.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fitness Devices Properly Track Workouts}: Flexin' will assume that data provided by fitness devices is correct.
    Hazards that stem from false data originating from workout devices are out of scope for this document.
    \item \textbf{Users Understand Exercises They Attempt}: Flexin' assumes that users will not attempt workouts that they are either physically incapable of or do not understand proper form of.
    Physical hazards that stem from improper workouts are out of scope for this document.
    \item \textbf{Users Have Positive Intentions}: Flexin' assumes that users want to learn about compounding and build positive habits.
    If the user has negative intentions, Flexin' will not be able to provide value.
    \item \textbf{Third-Party Health Platforms Remain Available}: Flexin' assumes that the health-data APIs it depends on (e.g., Health Connect, manufacturer SDKs) remain supported and keep their documented behaviour.
    Hazards from these platforms being shut down or changing their terms of use are out of scope; temporary outages and malformed responses are still analyzed.
    \item \textbf{The User's Device Is Not Compromised}: Flexin' assumes that the device running the app has not been rooted, jailbroken, or infected with malware.
    Hazards from a compromised operating system intercepting or changing app data are out of scope.
    \item \textbf{Cloud Infrastructure Provides Its Advertised Guarantees}: Flexin' assumes that its hosting and database provider meets its stated durability and encryption-at-rest guarantees.
    Application-level failures such as bad writes, failed syncs, and misconfigured access rules are still analyzed.
    \item \textbf{Virtual Currency Has No Monetary Value}: Flexin' assumes that its virtual currency and simulated portfolios cannot be converted to or withdrawn as real money.
    As a result, hazards involving real financial loss to users are out of scope.
    \item \textbf{Users Provide Truthful Personal Information}: Flexin' assumes that the height, weight, fitness level, and health conditions users enter are accurate to the best of their knowledge.
    \item \textbf{Users Keep Their Credentials Private}: Flexin' assumes that users do not deliberately share their login credentials with others.
    Account takeover through stolen or leaked credentials is still analyzed.
\end{itemize}
