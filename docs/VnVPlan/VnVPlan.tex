\section{Plan}

This section outlines the plan to verify and validate Flexin' across its project development lifecycle.
The plan will detail the roles and responsibilities of the team, and will specify the approach to verify the SRS document, design and implementation, as well as how to verify the VnV itself.
Lastly, it will describe the automated tools and software validation framework that the team plans on using.

\subsection{SRS Verification}

The SRS will be verified to ensure that all requirements detailed in the document are reasonable, accurate, understandable and complete.
The bullet list below describes the different avenues in which the SRS may be reviewed.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Submission to TA:} The SRS will be submitted to our course TA, who will thoroughly read and analyze the document and provide feedback where needed.
  \item \textbf{Peer Review:} Classmates will review the SRS document and ensure its content makes sense from a course standpoint.
  \item \textbf{Presentation to Supervisor:} The team may meet with the project supervisor to ensure that the requirements described in the SRS are high quality and in line with what they expect to be delivered.
\end{itemize}

If any problem are found with the SRS, the team will utilize our issue board to keep track and fix them.
Once development begins, these issues may also coincide with a bugfix.

\subsection{Design Verification}

Design verification will begin after the submission of the Design Document on November 16th.
This verification will ensure that Flexin's architecture meets requirements and is implementable.
The bullet list below describes how the Design Document may be reviewed.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Submission to TA:} The Design Document will be submitted to our course TA, who will thoroughly read and analyze the document and provide feedback where needed.
  \item \textbf{Peer Review:} Classmates will review the Design document and ensure its content makes sense from a course standpoint.
  \item \textbf{Presentation to Supervisor:} The team may meet with the project supervisor to ensure that the design is feasible from an implementation point of view.
\end{itemize}

Any problems with the Design Document will be handled through the teams issue board.


\subsection{Verification and Validation Plan Verification}

VnV plan verification will take place before the document is submitted by the team, as well as post submission by other members.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Team Review:} The team will review the VnV Plan ensuring that the defined tasks are feasible within the timeframe of the course and that the detail described in the document is appropriate.
  \item \textbf{Peer Review:} Classmates will review the VnV Plan and ensure its content makes sense from a course standpoint.
  \item \textbf{Presentation to Supervisor:} The team may meet with the project supervisor to ensure that the VnV Plan is feasible.
  \item \textbf{Submission to TA:} The VnV Plan will be submitted to the TA for grading and feedback.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implementation Verification}

Implementation verification ensures that the system, as built, correctly implements the SRS and design document.
This will be done through a combination of the following:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Unit Testing and System Testing:} All functional and non-functional requirements will be verified through the tests described in the below sections of this document.
  \item \textbf{Static Analysis and Linting:} The team will enforce a coding standard using linters and static analyzers.
  \item \textbf{Code Review:} All code changes will require a pull request and approval from at least one team member.
  The team members will be looking for code correctness, standards, and proper error handling.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Automated Testing and Verification Tools}

The team plans on creating standard unit tests that will run during the CI stage of the code development lifecycle.
The framework used for the backend will most likely be JUnit, and for the frontend, Detox.
The team may also create integration tests and end-to-end tests to ensure that application functionality doesn't degrade in the grand scheme.
Code coverage will be top of mind while developing this test plan, and a tool to ensure that a minimum agreed threshold of 80\% coverage will be implemented.

\subsection{Software Validation}

The software validation step of the VnV plan will ensure that the near-final product lives up to its expectations.
This validation will be done from the perspective of the users and the stakeholders.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rev-0 Demonstration:} The Rev-0 demo will be used to ensure that the core functionality of Flexin' is up to the standards of what the supervisor expects.
  \item \textbf{Usability Tests:} The team plans on giving Flexin' to a select group of prospective users for a period of roughly 2 weeks.
  This will help the team understand what needs work, and what worked well for the users.
\end{itemize}
